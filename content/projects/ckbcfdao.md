The CKB Community Fund DAO Dashboard gathers everything about the CKB Community Fund DAO in one place: the governance rules, every proposal from discussion and voting to funding and milestone updates, the treasury balance and how much has been spent. It explains the three public stages (discussion on Nervos Talk, voting on Metaforo, execution with milestone reports) and links each rule back to its source, without changing it.

The dashboard is community-maintained by JackyLHH and open source.
