JoyGift is a decentralized gifting platform on the Nervos CKB blockchain, utilizing the RGB++ protocol to enable secure and scalable gifting of CKB tokens within the Bitcoin ecosystem. It allows users to send CKB tokens as gifts to friends, communities, or other users, fostering social engagement and crypto adoption. Built on CKB’s secure Layer 1 and UTXO-based Cell model, JoyGift provides a seamless, trustless, and user-friendly experience for sharing crypto assets in Web3.
