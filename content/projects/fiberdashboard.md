The Fiber Network Dashboard is the public statistics page of the Fiber Network, CKB's payment channel network. It shows the number of active nodes and channels, the CKB liquidity locked in channels (total, minimum, maximum, average and median), how channels are shared between nodes, and how each figure changed from the week before. It is maintained by the Fiber team and open source.
