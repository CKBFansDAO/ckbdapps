CKBA (Common Knowledge Base Association) is a Swiss Verein: a membership-based non-profit governed by its contributors. It brings the people and efforts that have shaped CKB from the start under one coordinating body.

There are two ways to join. General membership is for CKB supporters who want a more direct way to take part. Contributing membership is for developers, researchers, infrastructure operators and community members who have contributed to CKB, and lets them shape the association's direction. The site lists the current contributing members.
