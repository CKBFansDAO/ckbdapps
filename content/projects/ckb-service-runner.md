CKB Service Runner (CSR) runs beside a fully synced CKB full node and gives its operator a local dashboard: sync status, node metrics, peers and a public node ranking published by the CSR Network Monitor. In a later phase the runner executes on-chain services and contracts, timed or on demand, for tips paid in CKB, turning spare node capacity into a revenue layer for node operators. Open source, Linux and Windows, testnet and mainnet.
