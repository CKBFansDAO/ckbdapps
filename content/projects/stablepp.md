Stable++ is a pioneering decentralized protocol on the Nervos CKB blockchain, built on the RGB++ layer to enhance Bitcoin's scalability and programmability. As the first over-collateralized stablecoin protocol on CKB, Stable++ allows users to mint BUSD stablecoins by over-collateralizing BTC assets, bringing stability and liquidity to the Bitcoin and RGB++ ecosystems. It leverages CKB's Proof-of-Work (PoW) consensus and Cell model, combined with the RGB++ protocol's Bitcoin UTXO to CKB Cell mapping, to provide secure and scalable stablecoin issuance and trading.
