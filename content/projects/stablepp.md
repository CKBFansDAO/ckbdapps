Stable++ issues RUSD, an over-collateralized stablecoin built on RGB++. Users lock BTC or CKB as collateral and mint RUSD in return. Because RUSD is an RGB++ asset, it can move between CKB and Bitcoin with RGB++ leap. The vaults and liquidation modules run as CKB scripts.
