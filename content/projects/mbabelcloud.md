mBabel Cloud provides real-time captions and reference translations for Chinese–English meetings. It supports custom vocabulary and preferred translations, as well as saving, renaming and exporting meeting text. Users can sign in with a wallet or a username account, purchase translation time with CKB, or redeem a code.

mBabel Cloud 为中英双语会议提供实时字幕和参考翻译，支持自定义术语及中英文对应翻译，以及会议文本的保存、重命名和导出。用户可以通过钱包或用户名账户登录，使用 CKB 购买翻译时长，也可兑换时长码。
