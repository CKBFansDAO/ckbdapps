Scryve (formerly InkHaven) is a publishing platform for long-form essays. Writers connect a CKB, EVM or Bitcoin wallet to publish, and readers tip them directly on-chain, with no subscription or NFT mint in between. Each article is archived permanently on Arweave with a cryptographic authorship seal, so who wrote it, and when, can be verified later.

The editor supports Markdown, code blocks, images and embeds. Scryve is built by LusoCryptoLabs, who also published Scryve Reads, a demo of pay-per-section reading over Fiber.
