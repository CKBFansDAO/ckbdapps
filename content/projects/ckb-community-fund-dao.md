The CKB Community Fund DAO is a fund started by the Nervos community and contributed to by the community and the Nervos Foundation. It pays community members for work around the CKB ecosystem, such as development, events and content. This site is where its votes happen, on a space Metaforo runs for the DAO.

A proposal moves through three public stages: discussion on Nervos Talk, a vote here, and execution with progress reports to the community. To vote, you bind your Nervos DAO address to your account, and your current deposit sets your voting weight. Starting a vote takes at least 100,000 CKB in the Nervos DAO, and a vote runs for seven days.

The current thresholds and payment rules are in the DAO's published rules. Anyone can read the proposals and results here without a deposit.
