Bitcoin Light is the new name of CKBull, the CKB mobile wallet built by Peersyst. It holds BTC and CKB in one app, sends Lightning payments, and manages RGB++ assets. Keys stay on your device, protected by biometrics or a PIN, and the wallet is available on Android and iOS.
